HealthConnect Clinic · Weeks 4–7

HealthConnect Clinic: predicting missed appointments

Built a model to flag patients likely to miss an appointment, so the clinic can contact them first. Compared a Logistic Regression baseline with a Random Forest, then changed the decision threshold to catch more of the real no-shows.

75%
no-shows flagged (up from 60%)
0.665
best F1 score
12 of 18
features selected

Before modelling I defined the problem: the target, the features, a patient-level split so no patient appears in both training and test data, median filling for missing values, and the risk of bias by gender and age. Recall was the main measure because missing a no-show costs the clinic more than a false alarm.
